“Expertise you can see”

LinkedIn and the other channels run by people who do this for a living: useful content that shows your expertise without taking up your time.

  • Visibility on ChatGPT and AI Overviews, monthly report

* AI and automation

“Repetitive work, on autopilot”

Chatbots, quotes and automatic workflows that answer clients and handle repetitive tasks, even outside office hours, so your time goes to client work.

  • Website chatbot that answers common questions and collects enquiries

FAQ

Q: How much does it cost?

A: Fixed prices, no surprises. On the call, after seeing your firm, we tell you what it costs and how long it takes, before you sign anything.

Q: Do I have to take all the services?

A: No. We start with what you need most, for example the website with Google visibility, and add the rest whenever you like.

Q: How do you protect my clients' data?

A: Hosting in Switzerland, privacy and cookies compliant with the FADP, and a written policy on the use of data in AI tools. For a professional firm it is also something you will want to explain to your clients.

Q: Who writes the content?

A: We prepare it with the help of AI, and a person reviews every text before it goes online. You tell us whether it represents you.

Q: How long does it take to go online?

A: About ten working days from the call. Most of the time goes into collecting photos and information about the team, which is why we only ask you for a ten-minute form.
